## Palette chosen

Midnight Local, because the download page is a dark product page and the mark uses one warm orange.

- Background `#0B0D12`
- Surface `#12151C`
- Border `#232733`
- Text `#E8EAF0`
- Muted `#9AA1B2`
- Accent `#FF7A45`
- Success `#3DD68C` is recorded and not used on this page, so the page keeps one accent.

Paper Trust (`#2F5BEA` on a light page) and Siam Teal (`#0F9D8A` with `#F2B33D`) stay unused alternatives.
